Login & Role Routing
Every user enters through Google Sign-In. The app checks the domain, looks up their role in the database, and routes them to the correct view. Roles are pre-seeded for Admin / Dean / Coordinator / Counselor. Teachers and students arrive via CSV import.

Period Mode & Entry Points
The app detects the current time automatically and switches modes. No manual toggling required. The period determines which reporting flows are available and what the default urgency level is.

Report Flow & Auto-Escalation
How a teacher's report becomes an incident. Context tags determine urgency automatically — no coordinator judgment call needed for the initial level. Counselor destination triggers a 10-minute confirmation timer before auto-escalating.

Coordinator 6-Step Workflow & Resolution
Once an incident is open, the coordinator runs a structured 6-step workflow. Steps 1, 2, and 6 generate communications automatically. At any point — before step 6 — any staff member can mark the student found, which immediately closes remaining steps as N/A and resolves the incident.
